# ivan yakovlev

speech processing researcher & engineer · barcelona

I work on speech models — speaker recognition, multilingual TTS, streaming
ASR, and translation. Currently building voice AI at [Palabra AI](https://palabra.ai);
previously spent five years at ID R&D Inc., where I designed the
[ReDimNet](/publications/) architecture and led our entries in the VoxCeleb
and SASV challenges.

## experience

**Palabra AI** — Senior Research Engineer
*Mar 2025 – present · Barcelona*

Solo-built a production zero-shot multilingual streaming TTS for 8+ languages
with voice cloning, now the core of Palabra's voice AI platform. Designed a
$0.10/hour pseudo-labeling pipeline (10–50× below industry standard) that
collected 200K+ hours across 20 languages. Architecting end-to-end
speech-to-speech translation. Researched a streaming LLM-ASR model
(~640 ms latency) that surpasses conformer-transducer baselines in most
domains. Designed ReDimNet2 — second-generation ReDimNet with improved
scalability via time-pooled dimension reshaping.

**ID R&D Inc.** — Sr. ML Engineer, Speech Processing
*Sep 2019 – Feb 2025 · Barcelona (remote)*

Invented Reshape Dimensions Network (ReDimNet) — 60% faster, 35–50% smaller,
and 15–25% more accurate than ResNets / ECAPA-TDNN / CAM++. Built an
automated pipeline that produced a 200k-identity dataset, doubling ASV
performance across multiple domains. Designed a subnetwork approach for
ResNet-style architectures, improving downstream task metrics by 50%+.
Achieved 4× speaker-verification training speedup with PyTorch + TPU.
Top placements in VoxCeleb (1st open-track 2022 & 2023) and SASV challenges,
contributing to three publications.

**EPAM Systems** — Software Engineer
*Feb 2017 – Mar 2019 · Saint Petersburg*

AR/VR applications integrating deep-learning vision models with Unity3D.
